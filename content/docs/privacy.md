This Privacy Policy explains how fewer ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the fewer web application at [fewer.directory](https://fewer.directory) and related services.

By using fewer, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Our Commitment to Privacy

fewer is designed around a simple principle: **your directory data stays yours**. The app runs entirely in your browser. Your imported directories, graphs, edits, and exported files are processed locally on your device and are not sent to our servers by default.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising trackers. We do not run telemetry that spies on how you use the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

Because fewer is primarily a client-side application, we collect very little data. Depending on how you use the app, we may handle the following:

### Information You Provide (Optional)

- **Account information**: If you create an account, we store your email address and authentication credentials (managed securely by Supabase Auth).
- **Saved graphs**: If you sign in and save directories, the graph data you explicitly save is stored in our database so you can access it across devices.
- **Share links**: If you create share links, the graph data you choose to share is stored and served publicly to anyone with the link.

### Information Collected Automatically (Minimal)

- **Hosting logs**: Standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) may be collected by our hosting provider to operate and secure the service.
- **Authentication logs**: If you sign in, authentication providers may log standard sign-in events for security.

### Information You Choose to Provide for Optional Features

- **Cloud connections**: If you opt in to connect third-party services (GitHub, Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Azure DevOps, Azure Blob), we access those services on your behalf through OAuth to list and import content. We only use the minimum access needed to fetch the trees you request. We do not store the contents of your connected accounts.
- **Watch digest**: If you enable watch digests, we store the public file index URLs you add and send you a daily email when those indexes change.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the fewer service
- Allow you to save, access, and share your graphs across devices
- Send you watch digests you have requested
- Improve, secure, and troubleshoot the service
- Respond to your requests and support inquiries

## 4. How We Share Information

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

- **With your consent**: For example, when you create a share link, the graph you share is made publicly accessible.
- **With service providers**: We use trusted providers (such as Supabase for authentication and database, and our hosting provider) to operate the service. These providers process data only to perform their functions for us.
- **For legal reasons**: If required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of fewer and its users.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- Your graph data that you save is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Account credentials are handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard security practices.
- We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Saved graphs and accounts**: We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.
- **Deletion**: You can delete saved graphs at any time. If you delete your account, we will delete your saved data within a reasonable period. Contact us to request deletion of your data.

## 7. Use by Children

fewer is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our hosting providers are located. By using the service, you consent to such processing.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including under the GDPR and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to receive a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post a notice. Your continued use of fewer after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach us through the [fewer GitHub repository](https://github.com/qvesera/fewer) by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers.

- **Individual contributors:** include the sign-off line in every commit

  Agreement, and confirm it in your pull request description. The DCO-style

  replacing it with your name and email.
- **Corporate contributors:** having an authorized representative open the
  pull request with the sign-off line, or contact the maintainer to execute a
  corporate CLA covering the contributor(s) and the employing entity.
